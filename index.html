Our story · Rooted in Guilin

Crafted in Guilin, inspired by nature.

Set among the graceful karst mountains and rivers of Guilin, Guangxi, Guilin KY draws inspiration from a place where natural beauty has shaped generations of craftsmanship. The calm lines of the landscape remind us that beautiful design is often found in balance, proportion and thoughtful detail.

For premium wooden hangers, the material is where quality begins. We value careful wood selection, attractive natural grain and suitable material characteristics, then bring these qualities forward through shaping, sanding, polishing and finishing. Each detail contributes to the way a garment is supported and presented.

Alongside wooden hangers, we offer metal and plastic hanger options and custom suit garment bags, helping global fashion brands, boutiques and hospitality buyers develop coordinated presentation solutions.

From the landscapes of Guilin to wardrobes around the world — thoughtful materials, careful workmanship and a personal approach to every project.
